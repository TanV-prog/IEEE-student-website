About our IEEE student branch

We are a student chapter of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, the world's largest technical professional organization. Our branch brings students together to learn by building: hardware, software and everything in between.

Throughout the year we run workshops, hackathons, project clinics and talks, open to students from every department.

WorkshopsHands-on sessions on embedded systems, web and AI.
CompetitionsHackathons and design challenges with real prizes.
MentorshipSeniors and alumni guiding projects and careers.
